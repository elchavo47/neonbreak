NeonBreak v4.2.1 BUILD FIX

1) BlockNode.normalTexture renamed to baseTexture because SKSpriteNode now exposes normalTexture, causing stored-property override/ambiguity errors.

No gameplay/balance logic changed in this build-fix package.
