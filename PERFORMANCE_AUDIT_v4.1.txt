Neon Break v4.1.0 - Performance Refactor

- BallNode SKShapeNode -> SKSpriteNode
- Ball texture is cached once per scene
- Removed per-ball core/halo shape nodes for Pulse
- Precise collision disabled for normal arcade ball physics
- Removed didSimulatePhysics per-frame velocity rewrite
- Velocity maintenance now runs only ~4x/sec and after block hits when needed
- Aim line reuses one SKShapeNode; only its path changes
- CoreHaptics per-hit pattern/player creation removed; UIKit feedback generators reused
- Hit sound playback is throttled and AVAudioPlayer pools reduced
- No bounce/trajectory prediction; only one straight dashed direction indicator
- Difficulty no longer reads server tuning
- Local curve: 2 blocks through lvl 12, 3 through lvl 39, 4 through lvl 89, 5 after lvl 90
- Local HP: ~1 + level/4 with small late-game jitter
- Balls/backgrounds are temporarily directly usable without purchase
