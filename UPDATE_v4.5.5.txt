NeonBreak iOS v4.5.5 — Avatar Crop Reliability Fix

- Crop source is normalized/downsampled once before editing (max 2048px).
- Drag/zoom no longer re-renders the original huge camera photo on every gesture frame.

- Cached avatar is used on Profile and Home screens, so avatar does not depend on internet.
